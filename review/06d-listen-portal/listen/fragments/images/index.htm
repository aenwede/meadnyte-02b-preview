Listen/Fragments/Images

Minds in turmoil

Emanating chaotic images

Emotions unleashed

Wreaking untold havoc

The genesis of the terror

 

Apprehension grips the earth

Hearts hang in suspense

A sudden ennui permeates all

Conjuring into existence

A chilling state of limbo

 

Formless, yet displacing the visible

Reeking of mind-boggling odors

Radiating an unearthly chill

That scorches body and soul

The terror stalks the earth

 

Hate!

Ugly specter

Perverted psychic projections

Issuing out of deranged minds

Essence of the image . . . the Terror
