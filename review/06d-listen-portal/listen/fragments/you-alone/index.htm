You Alone

Listen to You Alone with its poem and artwork.

Listen/Fragments/You Alone

You alone!

Honey, it's you alone

That can make me feel

The way I do

You alone!

Can knock my heart

Out of the strong grip

Of its keepers

You alone!

Can turn me inside out

Disorienting me

From my very self

I feel like a feather

Tossed about by the winds

Lost to the world

I am lost to myself

I live just to be with

You alone!
