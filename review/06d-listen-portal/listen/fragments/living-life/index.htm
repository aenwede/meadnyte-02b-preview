Living Life

Listen/Fragments/Living Life

Before the day

Is the dark night

Mother of the morning light

Raise your head

Look straight ahead

Search for the pin-point of light

Ignore the darkness around you

Look within, for therein

Is the beginning of the dawning

The darkness cannot last forever

Neither will the day

But like the days of summer

Your nights can be shorter

And your days longer

Life . . . is what you make of it!
