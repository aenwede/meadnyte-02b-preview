Listen/Fragments/The Day You'll Finally Be Mine

My Island of Patmos

Came into existence

The very day I met you

On it I know

Neither friend nor foe

But peace of mind eludes me

For your presence is absent

And I pine away

Longing for the day

You will finally be mine
