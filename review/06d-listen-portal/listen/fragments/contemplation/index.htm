Listen/Fragments/Contemplation

When in the midst of trouble

Emotions well up, and overflow

Feelings block out perceptions

And the intellect

Takes a forced break

Futile in its efforts to be heard

Then . . .

Is the time . . .

To stop . . .

And take a deep breath . . .

And start

To control and to change

The source of the upset

By listening to the heartbeat

Of your innermost self

And . . .

The silence . . .

Of the universes.....within!
