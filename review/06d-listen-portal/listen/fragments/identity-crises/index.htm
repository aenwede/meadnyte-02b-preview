Identity Crises

Listen to Identity Crises with its poem and artwork.

Listen/Fragments/Identity Crises

A black void

Seemingly without boundaries

Seeps through and smothers

All the senses of mind and body

At the center of the heart

A numbness begins to grow

Suppressing the realization of self

Devouring the cold light of logic

Mother of identity

Estranged from the environment

In the midst of alien activities

The mind is enmeshed

In a mind boggling sequence

Of over-reacting and under-reacting

Building up to an electric frenzy

That erupts with a crashing explosion

Into an enigmatic identity crises

Paralysis ensues

The mind is severed from the body

Impotent in its efforts

To instill order and coordination

Over the activities of the body

The intellect begins to roam

Engaging the outermost limits of reason

Unable to contact the physical universe

Futile in expressing itself

Lacking control of the neuro systems

Isolated from the command center

Body parts itch and twitch

Executing meaningless and erratic motions

Frantically attempting to communicate

Residual thought impulses from the mind

Creating only a distraught state of confusion

Attempted mutiny

The subconscious engages

Taking control in stages

Seeking dominance over the conscious mind

Breaking through its restraining binds

Establishing an alternate identity

Weary, the ego battles to grasp

The essence of its identity

Trapped in the vortex of the crises

Emotional responses become unpredictable

Energy patterns disperse and disintegrate

The universes seem to collapse

As the reference points become unstable

Changing aspects and positions

Creation is an impossible exercise

Not having any tangible substance

Dissolved in the void of identity crises.
