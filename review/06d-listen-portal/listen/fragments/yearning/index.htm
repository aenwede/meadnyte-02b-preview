I craved to love

I yearned to be loved

I turned every nook and cranny

In search of true love

You hungered to love

You pined to be loved

You travelled from the bottom of the sea

To the top of the mountains

In search of true love

I saw it

You saw it

That fleeting and elusive reality

Winged like a butterfly

When we found each other

I love you

You love me

We found true love

Our happiness is complete

Now that we are together
