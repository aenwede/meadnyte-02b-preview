The Oracle

Listen to The Oracle with its poem and artwork.

Listen/Fragments/The Oracle

Be silent!

Raging rivers of waters

Waves that froth and crash

Ancient habitat of Leviathan

Be still and harken

Thundering and crackling clouds

Riding upon the wings

Of whirling and twirling winds

Upon an endless journey.

Be still and harken

Erupting infernos of molten rock

Spewn from the bowels

Of the rumbling and hurtling planet

Spinning stories of awe

Be still and harken

The harbinger of good tidings

Crusader against any evil

That destiny may have

Waiting in the distant future

I am the Oracle!

Ancient sage of eons gone by

Sole advisor of your elders

Keeper of the sacred truths

Gods, demigods, and men seek

Guardian of immortality

Executor of justice

Invincible power of the universes

I am the Oracle!

Behold! . . . I am speaking.
