In Transit — Take Two

Listen to In Transit — Take Two with its poem and artwork.

Listen/Fragments/In Transit — Take Two

The sound

Of the howling wind

Is all around me

And I feel

A gust of cold air

Whip across my exposed face

Turning around

I take refuge inside

The wind shelter

Wind shelter?

Funny name

The structure itself

Needs to be sheltered

From the wind

Time seems to stand still

The waiting eternal

These buses are no good

They do not come on time

If they come at all

Bravely I grit my teeth

And huddle inside my jacket

An isolated object . . .

In Transit!
