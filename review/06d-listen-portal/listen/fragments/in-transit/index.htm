In Transit

Listen to In Transit with its poem and artwork.

Listen/Fragments/In Transit

Hear the wind howling

A gust of cold air

Whips across my exposed face

Time seems to stand still

Eternity is here

And this ordeal has no end

In a city of Mercedes Benz's

And cars of all shapes and sizes

Where a walking man

Is an unusual sight

I feel like the only one

Using a Leggedes Benz

The buses do not come in time

If they do come at all

The waiting is endless

Gritting my teeth

And huddling inside my jacket

I put on a bravado

I do not feel at all

But once inside the bus

The feeling of isolation disappears

As I become one with a multitude

Of people who like me

Have nothing but Leggedes Benz's
