The Universe!

Vast, open, unlimited

A void . . . delineated

By events in time

Made visible . . .

By the creation of matter

The Universe!

Matter, Energy, Time, without

Thoughts, and Postulates, within

Creating and Recreating

Forever indestructible in its continuity

An undefined void

Seeking to be filled

Repulsive and attractive

In its creating-uncreating processes

Ageless and infinite
