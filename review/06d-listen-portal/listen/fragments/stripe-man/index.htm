Stripe Man

Listen to Stripe Man with its poem and artwork.

Listen/Fragments/Stripe Man

The stripe man

Dresses in stripes

From head to toe

Wears a hat of stripes

Talks in stripes

Looks in stripes

Lives within stripes

His hair

Is like stripes

On his scalp

And over his skin

He truly is

The stripe man

He tapers away gently

And disappears into nothingness

Look to the Rainbow

Within which lives

The stripe man

In a make-believe world

Of his own creation
