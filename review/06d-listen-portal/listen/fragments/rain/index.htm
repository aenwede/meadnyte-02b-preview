Rain

Listen to Rain with its poem and artwork.

Listen/Fragments/Rain

Bear witness!

Children of nature bear witness

The clouds have gathered

In solemn congregation

The big clouds and the small clouds

Of all shapes and sizes

The mood is dark

The sun has taken shelter

Awed by the gathering

Lightning has been summoned

To lead with its flashes

Thunder follows behind

Kettle drums roaring

The procession is on the move

Cascades of water descend

The thirsty earth drinks

All the children of nature rejoice

A great applause is heard

From rooftops, from dirt

And from paved surfaces

A show of appreciation

For the rain
