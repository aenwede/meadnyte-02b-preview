Faulting

Listen to Faulting with its poem and artwork.

Listen/Fragments/Faulting

Too many people

Want to change the world

They find faults

In the way the system is run

They find faults

In the products of the system

Yeah, they find faults

In everything, everywhere

In every which way

Nothing, but one thing, is sacrosanct

No other selves, but themselves

Is immune from their meticulous faulting

Must be a shame

People never learn from history

The past is the mother of the present

The present the mother of the future

History must repeat itself

Surely it is true

Wisdom cannot be found in books

But yet the wise will glean

Much wisdom from some books

Could not be in vain

That the sages of ages past

Warned . . .

That . . .

The fault . . .

Lies not in the stars

But in ourselves

For we are the culprits

Of the faults we find

In the system
