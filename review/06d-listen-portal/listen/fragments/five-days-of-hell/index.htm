5 Days Of Hell

Listen/Fragments/5 Days Of Hell

While you were gone

I threw a ball

I invited one and all

But it was no fun

While you were gone

The sky was overcast

The days stood fast

All nature seemed to mourn

While you were gone

Nothing remained the same

The sane became insane

Misery and sadness found fame

Joy and delight became lame

Life stopped being a game

Living suddenly was a pain

Mind discipline was but in vain

As the heart awaited your return
