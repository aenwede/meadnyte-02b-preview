Procrastination

Listen to Procrastination with its poem and artwork.

Listen/Fragments/Procrastination

Wait!

Today is a good day

But there is always another day

It could be done now

But could as well be done later

There really is no deadline

Just a subject of whim

Taking it easy is the game

Yet there is no gain

The longer it takes

The harder it gets

Eventually it will get done

By then the joy will be gone

The appreciation will be less

The effort just a jest

So stop procrastinating

Get on the ball, start doing

The wait is over
