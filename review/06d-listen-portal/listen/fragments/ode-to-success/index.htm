Fall . . . . . . . . . . and rise

Fall . . . . . . and rise

Fall down . . . and get up

Get up . . . Get up . . .

Get up and fight

Fight for what you believe in

Do not ever let up

Feel the discomfort

And the agony of failure

Feel it wash over you

Drown you and overwhelm you

Soak it in, watch it carefully

Draw strength from its depths

Surmount its oppressive weight

And Rise . . . . .

Like the rising sun

With full glory and exhilaration

Rise up… Stand high

Reach up… Reach out

Embrace the skies

High above the clouds

Feel the warmth and joy

As you rise up

Never to fall again
