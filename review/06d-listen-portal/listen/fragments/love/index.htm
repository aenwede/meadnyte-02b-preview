Love

Listen to Love with its poem and artwork.

Listen/Fragments/Love

Here I stand

Enveloped in a misty world

Of doubts that assail me

And fears that stab at me

From all directions

I turn around for help

There is none can comfort me

For my plight is strange

Even to the wisest of those

Who around me have gathered

Victim of a strange affliction

Blown about by winds

That bear shafts of agony

On their very soft wings

Cursed by Cupid to remain

On this strange island

Yet a smile on my face remains

Voices that rise and fall

Ringing in mourning notes

Of distress and grief

Carrying messages

Of deceitful aberrations

Yet to come to pass

Impress themselves upon me

But I give not heed to them

For I live

In the knowledge

And the understanding

Of what I am

For I am . . . Love
