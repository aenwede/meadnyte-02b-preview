Listen/Fragments/Santa Monica Beachfront Walk At 6.00 pm

Waves rolling in and out

A pervading whooooosh

Dominates and permeates

Silent footfalls . . .

People walking to and fro

Thumping footfalls . . .

People jogging along

The tinkling of keys

As arms pendulate

From their shoulder sockets

Loud roars from overrun engines

The gentle purring of luxury cars

An occasional voice impinges

The unsilent silence

Of Santa Monica Beachfront walk

Punctuated by the total absence

Of singing birds

At 6 o'clock p.m. on a spring evening
