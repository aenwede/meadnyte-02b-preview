A Muddy Little Lesson

The Story

One mossy rock.More than one way to learn.

Mother Sheep and her lambs—Baffle-Fluff, Wiggle-Wump, and Nwaybulakur—meet a mossy rock with a stubborn lesson to teach.

While Baffle-Fluff discovers the answer the muddy way, Nwaybulakur watches closely and learns that wisdom does not always require repeating someone else’s fall.
