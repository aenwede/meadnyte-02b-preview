Cookie Policy

The House currently operates without advertising pixels, behavioral analytics, cross-site trackers, or nonessential cookies on meadnyte.com.

Effective August 2, 2026Last updated August 2, 2026

On this page

  1. Current state
  2. Session storage
  3. External providers
  4. Browser controls
  5. Future tracking

1. Current Meadnyte site state

As of the effective date, the Meadnyte website does not intentionally set nonessential cookies or load third-party advertising, cross-context behavioral advertising, social tracking pixels, or behavioral analytics.

No cookie-consent banner is displayed because the current site does not activate nonessential cookies or tracking. A banner that offers no meaningful choice would add friction without improving privacy.

2. Browser session storage

The landing-page subscriber collector may use session storage to remember limited source-attribution parameters during the current browser session. These can include utm_source, utm_medium, utm_campaign, and utm_content when they are present in the page address.

Session storage is local to the browser tab or session and normally expires when that session ends. It is used to understand how a visitor reached the collector, not to follow the visitor across unrelated websites.

3. External provider pages

When you choose a link to a Mailchimp-hosted signup, confirmation, unsubscribe, or preference page—or another external platform—that provider may use its own cookies, storage, logs, and security controls. Those technologies are controlled by the external provider and described in its policies.

4. Browser controls

You can clear site data through your browser settings. Blocking all storage may affect source attribution or some provider-hosted functionality, but the public Meadnyte rooms should remain generally accessible.

The current site does not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising. Accordingly, there is no such current activity for a Global Privacy Control or similar browser signal to opt out of.

5. Future nonessential technologies

Before Meadnyte activates nonessential analytics, advertising, profiling, or similar tracking, the House will:

  • identify the provider, purpose, information, duration, and recipients;
  • update this Cookie Policy and the Privacy Policy;
  • provide clear notice and preference controls where required;
  • keep nonessential technology inactive until a required consent is given; and
  • honor withdrawal and recognized browser preference signals as applicable.

Questions may be sent to social@meadnyte.com.
