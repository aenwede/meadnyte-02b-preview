Subscriber Preferences

Your preferences are personal to your subscriber record. Use the secure link in a Meadnyte email to change them.

Effective August 2, 2026Last updated August 2, 2026

1. Open your secure Preferences Center

Open any Meadnyte marketing email sent to you and select Manage Preferences in the footer. Mailchimp creates a subscriber-specific link that securely identifies the correct preference record. For your protection, Meadnyte does not publish a universal link that exposes or guesses subscriber identity.

Look for Manage Preferences near the unsubscribe link in the email footer.

2. What you may change

The customized House Preferences Center is intended to allow confirmed subscribers to:

  • update first name and optional last name;
  • select or remove Books, Poetry, Music, Film, Signals, and Everything;
  • save changes without leaving all House communications; and
  • reach the separate unsubscribe choice.

Changes may take a short time to appear in an automation already being processed. Meadnyte will apply them to future eligible messages as the provider records them.

3. Leave House communications

Select Unsubscribe in any marketing email to stop marketing communications. Meadnyte may retain a suppression record so your address is not unintentionally contacted again. A later return requires a legitimate new consent and confirmation process.

4. If the link does not work

Email social@meadnyte.com from the subscribed address and describe the change you want. Do not forward a personalized preference link to another person.

For access, correction, deletion, or other privacy rights beyond subscriber preferences, use the Data Request page.
