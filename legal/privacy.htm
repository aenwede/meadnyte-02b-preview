1. Scope and operator

This Privacy Policy applies to meadnyte.com, House-controlled subscriber communications, and direct privacy requests. “Meadnyte,” “House of Meadnyte,” “World of Meadnyte,” “House,” “we,” and “our” refer to the Meadnyte creative enterprise operating these services.

It does not govern websites, platforms, or services operated by others, including a provider page you visit through an external link. Those services publish their own privacy terms.

Contact: social@meadnyte.com
Postal address: 7095 Hollywood Boulevard, #846, Los Angeles, CA 90028

2. Information we collect

Information you provide

  • Subscriber information: email address, first name, optional last name, discovery interests, consent choice, subscription and preference status.
  • Communications: messages, replies, requests, attachments, and related contact information you send to the House.
  • Rights requests: information needed to locate records, verify identity, understand the request, and document our response.

Information collected through use

  • Source attribution: the Meadnyte page, form version, and limited campaign parameters such as utm_source, utm_medium, utm_campaign, and utm_content when present.
  • Technical records: hosting and security providers may process IP address, browser or device information, requested page, timestamps, referrer, and diagnostic or security events in ordinary server logs.
  • Email events: delivery, bounce, complaint, unsubscribe, preference, and—only where enabled and lawful—open or click events processed through the email provider.

We do not ask the public collector for payment information, government identification, precise location, health information, biometric information, or account passwords.

3. How we use information

  • to provide the site and requested House experiences;
  • to send double-opt-in confirmation and, after confirmation, approved Meadnyte communications;
  • to recognize interests and personalize subscriber content;
  • to maintain preferences, honor unsubscribe and suppression choices, and prevent duplicate or unauthorized delivery;
  • to answer communications and privacy requests;
  • to operate, secure, troubleshoot, audit, and improve the site and communications;
  • to protect House rights, visitors, subscribers, systems, and legal interests; and
  • to comply with applicable law and enforce governing terms.

Where applicable law requires consent, we rely on consent. For ordinary site operation, security, requested communications, and internal administration, we may also rely on performance of a requested service, legitimate interests, or legal obligations as applicable.

4. How information is disclosed

We do not sell personal information. We do not share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising. We do not disclose subscriber lists to unrelated parties for their own direct marketing.

We may disclose information to:

  • Service providers: including Mailchimp for subscription forms, consent, audience management, preferences, and email delivery; hosting, infrastructure, security, and professional-service providers acting for Meadnyte.
  • Collaborators and personnel: authorized House personnel who need the information to perform assigned work and are subject to appropriate duties.
  • Legal or safety recipients: when reasonably necessary to comply with law, legal process, protect rights and safety, investigate misuse, or enforce terms.
  • Business transaction recipients: if a relevant business or asset is reorganized, financed, transferred, or acquired, subject to appropriate confidentiality and notice where required.
  • At your direction: when you ask or authorize us to disclose information.

Mailchimp is operated by Intuit. Its processing may include infrastructure outside your state or country and is also governed by its applicable privacy terms.

5. Cookies, storage, and tracking

As of the effective date, meadnyte.com does not load advertising pixels, behavioral analytics, cross-site trackers, or other nonessential cookies. The landing-page collector may use browser session storage to retain limited source-attribution parameters during the current browsing session. This information normally expires when the session ends.

External provider pages, including Mailchimp-hosted confirmation or preference pages, may use their own cookies and storage. See the Cookie Policy for details. If Meadnyte later introduces nonessential tracking, we will update the policy and deploy appropriate notice and consent controls before activation where required.

Because the current site does not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising, there is no such activity for a browser opt-out signal to change. Where a legally recognized preference signal applies to a future covered practice, Meadnyte will honor it as required.

6. Retention and security

We retain personal information only as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described here, including maintaining consent and suppression records, providing communications, resolving requests, securing the system, protecting rights, and meeting legal obligations. Unsubscribe and suppression records may be retained to ensure that a person who leaves is not inadvertently contacted again.

We use administrative, technical, and organizational measures intended to protect information. No transmission or storage method can be guaranteed completely secure. Please do not send sensitive information that we have not requested.

7. Your choices and rights

  • Subscription: confirm only if you wish to receive communications.
  • Preferences: use the personalized Manage Preferences link in a Meadnyte email to update name or interests.
  • Unsubscribe: use the unsubscribe link in any marketing email. We will honor the request and may retain a suppression record.
  • Privacy request: request access, correction, deletion, a copy, restriction, objection, or withdrawal of consent where applicable.

See Consumer Privacy Notice and Data Access, Correction & Deletion. We will not discriminate against you for exercising a privacy right protected by law.

8. Children’s privacy

The public site and subscriber program are intended for a general audience and are not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from a child under 13 through the collector. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us so we can review and take appropriate action.

9. International visitors

Meadnyte operates from the United States. Information may be processed in the United States and in other places where service providers operate. Privacy rights and transfer requirements vary by location. Where a law applies to our processing, we will use an available lawful basis and provide the rights required by that law.

10. Changes and contact

We may update this policy when practices, services, or laws change. The effective and last-updated dates identify the current version. Material changes will receive additional notice where required.

Questions or requests may be sent to social@meadnyte.com or mailed to Meadnyte, 7095 Hollywood Boulevard, #846, Los Angeles, CA 90028.
